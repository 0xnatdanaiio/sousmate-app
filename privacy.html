What we keep

When you create an account, we keep your email address, your business name, and the food safety records your staff enter in the app (fridge readings, fridge names, staff names, and any notes or reasons typed in).

Where it is stored

Records are stored with our hosting provider (Supabase, on Amazon Web Services) in Sydney, Australia. Each device also keeps its own copy.

How long

Records are kept for at least 4 years, as food safety records must be.
